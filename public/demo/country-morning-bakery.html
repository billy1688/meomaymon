From the Oven & the Kitchen

Baked from scratch with old-world recipes.

🍩

Sweet & Scratch-Baked

Cinnamon rolls (the local legend), bread pudding, cookies, muffins and fresh breads — plus ham-and-cheese rolls hot from the oven.

🥪

Breakfast & Sandwiches

Breakfast sandwiches on house-baked rolls, BLTs, pastrami and panini sandwiches — regulars swear the sandwich roll is "otherworldly."

🍲

Lunch & Take-and-Bake

Homestyle lunch specials — get the brisket when it's on — plus take-and-bake trays of lasagna, cheesy potatoes and chicken pot pies for home. Friday clam chowder is a local ritual.

☕

Coffee, Done Right

Now brewing Temple Coffee Roasters beans. Plant milks (soy, oat, almond) available — and a kids' play corner while you finish your cup in peace.
